Privacy Policy

Last updated August 23, 2026

Rez 3D ("we," "us," "our") provides 3D rendering and pre-leasing marketing services to real estate developers, primarily in Atlantic Canada. This policy explains what information we collect on this site and the tools linked from it, and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you use our pre-leasing calculator or fill out a form on this site, we collect the information you provide directly, which may include:

  • Your name, company, and email address
  • Project details you enter — city/market, unit count, pricing, opening date, and pre-leasing status

We also use standard web analytics and Google Ads conversion tracking to understand which ads and pages lead to form submissions. This does not include payment or financial account information — we do not collect that on this site.

How we use it

  • To generate the personalized report or plan you requested
  • To follow up with you about your project and our services
  • To measure the performance of our advertising campaigns

We do not sell your information to third parties.

Who we share it with

Form submissions are delivered to us through Web3Forms, a third-party form-processing service, solely to route your submission to our inbox. Google Ads receives anonymized conversion signals (that a submission occurred) but not the content of your submission. We do not share your information with anyone else except as required by law.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the information we hold about you at any time by emailing admin@rez3drenders.ca.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us at admin@rez3drenders.ca.
